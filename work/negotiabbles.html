Negotiabbles

iF Design Award Gold

Negotiabbles makes negotiation education more approachable for kids by turning everyday conflicts into playful, story-driven role-play. Through expressive characters, interactive choices, and real-time feedback, students can practice perspective-taking, communication, and decision-making in a safe environment before applying those skills in real life.

I led the team from problem framing to the final experience, shaping the core idea, interaction, visual direction, and how the whole world of Negotiabbles came together.
