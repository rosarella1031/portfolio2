Heartie

Red Dot Award 2025

Heartie is a child-centered health solution for kids aged 8–14 with heart conditions. Combining a 14-day ECG patch with a multi-device app ecosystem, Heartie bridges clinical monitoring with everyday care.

While most tools track only data, Heartie integrates AI for real-time arrhythmia detection, emergency alerts, and educational games that teach kids health knowledge. Through interstellar adventures, kids build physical and emotional self-care skills, while parents monitor heart data and share reports with doctors.

By blending medical safety, education, and psychological support, Heartie empowers families and reduces fear and isolation.
